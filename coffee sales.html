Data Visualization / Coffee Shop Sales

Coffee Sales
Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze coffee shop sales performance across products, payment methods, customer visits, and time patterns.

The Problem

The dataset contains over 3,600 raw sales transactions spanning a full year, including product type, payment method, transaction value, and customer card ID

making it difficult to identify sales trends, peak hours, top-performing products, and repeat customer behavior directly from the raw data.

The Solution

I developed an interactive Power BI dashboard that transforms the raw transaction data into clear and actionable visual insights. The dashboard allows users to explore:

  • Total Revenue across all transactions (85.83K)
  • Revenue by Coffee Type — from Latte and Americano with Milk to Espresso and Cocoa
  • Revenue and Order Count Trend by Product (with goal tracking)
  • Revenue Distribution by Quarter (Qtr 1–Qtr 4)
  • Cash vs Card Payment Comparison
  • Interactive Filtering by Year (2024 / 2025)
