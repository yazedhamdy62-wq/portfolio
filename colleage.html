Colleague:
College Guidance
Platform

Colleague is a web platform designed to help Egyptian high school students choose the right college based on their academic track, whether Literary, Science, or Mathematics. The platform provides students with clear and organized information about different colleges, including their requirements, programs, departments, and the tools or equipment they may need during their studies.

The Problem

High school students often struggle when choosing a college because they do not have enough information about the available options.

Important details such as college requirements, departments, study programs, required tools, and other essential information are often scattered across different websites and sources. This makes it difficult for students to compare colleges and understand which option is most suitable for them.

The Solution

Colleague brings essential college information together in one easy-to-use platform. It helps students discover and explore colleges that match their academic track and interests, while providing the information they need about each college, its requirements, programs, departments, and required tools. This makes the college selection process easier, clearer, and more informed.
