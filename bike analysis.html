Bike
Sales Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze bike sales performance across products, countries, customer segments, and different time periods.

The Problem

The dataset contains a large amount of sales information across different products, categories, countries, customers

and time periods, making it difficult to identify key sales and profitability insights directly from the raw data.

The Solution

I developed an interactive Power BI dashboard that transforms the available sales data into clear and actionable visual insights. The dashboard allows users to explore:

  • Total Revenue, Cost, and Profit
  • Revenue and Profit by Country
  • Product performance and profitability
  • Profit Margin and Revenue Share
  • Revenue by Customer Gender
  • Profit by Product Category
  • Revenue and Profit trends over time
  • Average Selling Price and Order Quantity
  • U.S. revenue distribution by state
  • Interactive filtering by Year, Month, and Product Category
