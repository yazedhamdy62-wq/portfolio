This project is a data analysis and visualization dashboard designed to analyze market sales performance across different product lines, cities, branches, genders, and months. The dashboard provides an overview of key business metrics such as total sales, gross income, cost of goods sold (COGS), and product performance, using interactive charts and visualizations.

The Problem

Businesses generate large amounts of sales data across different products, locations, branches, and customer groups.

Analyzing this raw data manually can make it difficult to understand which products generate the most income, which cities and branches perform better, how sales change over time, and how customer groups contribute to total sales.

The Solution

The project transforms the sales data into an interactive dashboard that presents the most important business insights in a clear and visual way.

It allows users to analyze sales and gross income by product line, city, branch, gender, and month, while also tracking key financial metrics such as total sales, COGS, and gross income.

This makes the data easier to understand and helps businesses identify sales trends, evaluate performance, and make data-driven decisions.
