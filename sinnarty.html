SINNARTY:
smart angling
ecosystem

SINNARTY is a smart fishing platform that sells smart fishing rods capable of detecting whether a specific fishing spot contains fish or not. The platform also helps users discover popular fishing spots where fish are commonly found, making it easier for them to choose the right location. In addition, SINNARTY offers fresh fish for sale directly to customers.

The Problem

Fishermen often spend a lot of time and effort trying different fishing spots without knowing whether fish are actually present.

Finding productive fishing locations can be difficult, and traditional fishing rods do not provide any information about fish activity underwater. This can result in wasted time, effort, and resources.

The Solution

SINNARTY combines smart fishing technology and location-based information to make fishing more efficient.

The smart fishing rod helps users detect whether fish are present at a specific spot, while the platform provides recommended fishing locations where fish are commonly found

SINNARTY also allows users to purchase fresh fish, creating a complete platform that connects smart fishing, fishing locations, and fish sales in one place.
