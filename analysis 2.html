Samsung:
Sales Analysis
Dashboard

This project is a data analysis and visualization dashboard designed to analyze Samsung's sales performance across different products, regions, quarters, and years. The dashboard provides insights into units sold, revenue, market share, 5G subscribers, product performance, and regional sales, allowing users to explore the data through interactive filters and visualizations.

The Problem

Samsung generates a large amount of sales data across different products, regions, years, and quarters, making it difficult to understand overall performance from raw data alone.

Without proper visualization and analysis, it can be challenging to identify top-performing products, high-revenue regions, sales trends, and customer preferences, which can slow down the decision-making process.

The Solution

The project transforms Samsung's raw sales data into an interactive Power BI dashboard that organizes and visualizes key business metrics in one place

The dashboard allows users to analyze sales, revenue, market share, product performance, regional performance, and 5G preferences using interactive charts, tables, and quarter filters.

This makes complex sales data easier to understand and helps businesses identify trends and make data-driven decisions.
