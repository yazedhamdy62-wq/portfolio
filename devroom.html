3D Dev
Room

The 3D Dev Room is an interactive 3D room you can look around in, right in the browser. It is a developer's space with a desk and a glowing monitor, a bookshelf, a carpet, a coffee cup, business cards, and posters of shows I love on the walls. I built it as a more personal way to show who I am. Instead of a flat page, visitors drag to look around and click the glowing objects to explore.
